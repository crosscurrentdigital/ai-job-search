# Freelance Marketplace Profiles

<!-- Drafted for supplemental income (roughly $500-$2,000 per project, not full-time hours) on Upwork and Freelancer.com. Update CLAUDE.md and re-draft here if scope or focus changes. -->

## Positioning

Small, fixed-price, well-scoped AI automation and adoption projects for small business owners - not open-ended retainers, not full-time-equivalent hours. Leads with the combination of (1) hands-on AI builder and (2) ten years of experience making new tools stick with non-technical people, which is the actual bottleneck most small-business AI projects hit.

## Suggested Package Tiers

Productized packages read better than an open hourly rate on these platforms, and match the "$500 here and there up to $2,000" target directly.

| Package | Price | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Quick Win** | $500 | One well-scoped automation or workflow fix (e.g. inbox/task triage setup, a single AI agent, one report or process automated). ~1 week. |
| **AI Adoption Audit + Implementation** | $1,000-$1,500 | Review current processes, identify 2-3 real AI opportunities, implement the highest-value one. ~1-2 weeks. |
| **Small Business AI Starter Package** | $2,000 | Audit + two implementations + a short handoff/training doc so the client's team can maintain and extend it themselves. ~2-3 weeks. |

Set an hourly rate too (most platforms require one even for fixed-price work) - $75-100/hr is a reasonable anchor given the caliber of shipped work, adjust as desired. Lead with the fixed-price packages regardless; the hourly rate is a fallback for scope-creep conversations, not the primary pitch.
